Welcome to CROP Barbershop

CROP is a real barbershop network in Astana. This student website presents one branch at 80 Uly Dala Avenue. It helps visitors find practical information before choosing a haircut or contacting the shop. The branch is presented as open daily from 08:00 to 22:00; visitors should confirm the schedule before travelling.

A place for personal style

What visitors can expect

The team provides men’s haircuts, children’s haircuts, beard care, styling and hair toning. A guest can discuss the desired result before the service begins. Clear communication matters because hair type and preferred length affect the final choice.
