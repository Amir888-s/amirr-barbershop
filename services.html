Services and Prices

CROP offers grooming services for different ages and styles. A standard men’s haircut is publicly listed from 5,000 ₸. The final price may depend on the barber level, hair length or selected branch, so the guest should ask for confirmation while booking. The word crop also describes a short hairstyle, but the shop provides more than one look.
